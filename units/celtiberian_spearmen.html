Description

These men are Celtic natives of Iberia who can also be recruited to support Carthaginian interests (If you're not at war with them!). They carry extremely long spears that are sharpened to a razor's edge, and are deadly from any angle. They are also heavily armoured, and very hard to kill, if you can get to them through their wall of spears! In common with other Iberian warriors, these men are very clever when concealing themselves in forests.
