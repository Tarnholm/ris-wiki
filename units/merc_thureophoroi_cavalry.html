Description

These mobile, javelin-wielding cavalry are protected by a classic Galatian thureos shield, while some additionally wear light armour and helmets. The Thureophoroi Cavalry were skilled riders, who could pepper the enemy from a distance or charge at unarmoured infantry with their spears.
