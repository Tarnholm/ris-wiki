Description

No cavalry dare attack these spearman, especially the experienced soldiers of this worthy band! Plenty of heads have been claimed by these men, and look no further than their spears to halt cavalry dead in its charge! On the flanks they prove their greatest worth as they maintain a watchful eye on the swift enemy horsemen fielded against their tribe.
