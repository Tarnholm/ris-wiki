Retinue

← wiki index · all traits · all factions

A character in RTR: Imperium Surrectum gathers a retinue — 1,116 followers, keepsakes, offices and animals that attach themselves to generals, governors and agents and travel with them. Where a trait is who a character has become, retinue is who and what he keeps around him, and members can be traded between characters who meet.

What counts as retinue a player sees — the tests that were measured

Unlike the trait file there is no Hidden flag here — the ancillary vocabulary has no concealment marker at all. The candidate tests:

Every entry is localised, so the name test selects nothing; the trigger test would drop 132 real, displayed followers the campaign script hands out (Hanno the Great among them). So all 1,116 are published, and an entry no trigger grants says so on its page.

How retinue works

  • A member arrives by trigger or by script. 1,799 triggers with 1,801 grant rules bring

984 of the 1,116; the other 132 come from the campaign script and events.

  • 787 members' acquisition is conditioned on the character's traits — the drunkard

attracts drinking companions, the scholar attracts philosophers. Those pages link both ways.

  • 275 are unique: one in the world at a time.
  • 843 exclude other members — rival followers who will not share a tent.

Where these answers come from

export_descr_ancillaries.txt declares every member, its effects and the triggers that grant it; display names and descriptions are text/export_ancillaries.txt (UTF-16LE) — the ancillaries' own text file, not export_vnvs.txt — keyed {token}, {token_desc}, {token_effects_desc}. Portraits are the mod's ui/ancillaries art, converted as-is. Effect attribute names are the engine's UI strings and exist in no mod text file, so they are rendered from the token. Where a description has no text entry the page says not determined rather than inventing one.
