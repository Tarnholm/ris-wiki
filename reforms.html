Reforms

Reforms are one-off changes to a faction's army. Each fires once, when its requirements are met, and from then on new units can be recruited, older ones are retired and some units already in the field are converted.

80 reforms. Open one to see what it takes and what it unlocks; every unit page links back to the reforms that open or close it.
