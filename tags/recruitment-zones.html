Recruitment zones

← reference tables · all regions · wiki index

A recruitment zone is a region tag that unlocks local troops. Hold a region inside the zone, build the military building the unit needs, and you may raise it — whoever you are. This is how RIS lets an empire field the men of the places it has taken rather than only its own.

Zones overlap, and the broader one steps aside: a unit gated on Greek is usually also gated on the region *not* being in a more specific Greek zone, so the generic hoplite appears where there is no local speciality and the local speciality appears where there is.

Where these answers come from, and what an unestablished effect means

What each value does is read off the clauses the mod conditions on it — a requirement on a building level, on a recruitment line, or on a numeric effect, including the ones reached through the building file's own aliases. Negated clauses count: blocking something is an effect. Where nothing in the mod conditions on a value, the entry says no effect established rather than describing what the word suggests.

170 zones, covering 436 distinct units. 155 unlock at least one unit; 168 cover at least one region. 15 unlock nothing: Alaudae early, Brigantian, Cyrenaica early, Equestris early, Ferrata early, Gallica early, Gemina early, Germanica early, Hispana early, Mesembrian, Olbian, Parthica early, Paterna early, Praetorian, Tomian. 2 are on no region at all: Brigantian, Chian. 1 is both.
