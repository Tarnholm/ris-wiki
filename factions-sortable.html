Factions

Every playable faction, sortable by culture or by how much it starts with. Units is the faction's own roster — what it can raise from its own buildings anywhere it holds a settlement. It excludes regional units, which are gated on holding the right province rather than on being anyone in particular: every faction has between 424 and 443 of those. Back to the wiki index.
