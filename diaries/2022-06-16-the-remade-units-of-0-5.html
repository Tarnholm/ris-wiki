The remade units of 0.5

2022-06-16 · Tarnholm

Hello everyone! It's been a while, hasn't it? The team has been busy working on the next release (0.5) for quite some time now, and finally, we can show some of what's coming. We have been working on many remade units as part of the RIS 0.5 update. Using new features that Feral has provided has allowed for unprecedented unit detail and fidelity. It's not hyperbole to call the new units revolutionary. Long gone are soldiers that all look the same. Instead, each soldier has different facial characteristics using Feral's new hair, facial hair and skin tones, and we didn't stop there. We use up to 7 distinctly different models per unit. That means up to 7 other shields, armours, etcetera, allowing for a battlefield unseen in the Rome 1 community. With that said, let us present the new Antigonid army, reforged by our growing graphics department under the watchful eyes of our outstanding historians.

At the time of our mods start, Macedon is no longer the centre of the world, but that doesn't mean its military might is not at the absolute top with units that can hold their own against the absolute best of the world. This is, of course, shown in the lavish armour worn by their elite troops, such as the Agema of the peltasts.

Thracian infantry:

Trallian infantry:

The Antigonids did, of course, not only use their own people when heading for wars. They employed mercenaries from neighbouring peoples, just as most other states at the time to fill out the ranks. Our Thracian infantry and Trallian infantry are two such units. If you look closely, you will notice that the Trallian unit uses both two-handed and one-handed weapons. Being part of the same unit, they have the same stats, but it adds flavour to the battlefield.
