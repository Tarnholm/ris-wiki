The Seleucid Empire

2022-09-10 · Tarnholm

Hello and welcome back! The release date for 0.5 is getting ever closer, which means yet another developer diary. The new update will come with plenty of new features, of which our all-new unit models and the brand new massive map are the most prominent. Speaking of massive, today's DD is about the largest Empire on our map, the Seleucids!

The Seleucid Empire covers the largest area of any starting factions in 270BC. From the border with the Antigonids in the west, across the central Anatolian cities to Cilicia, further still into the Syrian and Mesopotamian heartland, then onto the vast Iranian region, all the way to the ends of the civilised lands and the borders of India.

This is not only a massive undertaking for any player, be it a novice or an experienced one, in terms of stabilising what you own but also because of the multitudes of challenges that face you on your borders, where dozens of other factions await to exploit your weakness. From the very start, you will be at war with Bithynia (the historical war between Antiochus and the Northern Alliance of Bithynia, Heraclea Pontica and other city-states), but others may follow…

You control a heterogeneous empire. Dozens of different cultures live with and by another, and maintaining public order will be a recurring task across your campaign. And as per the historian Nymphis of Heraclea, restoration or even just maintaining the borders of Seleucus' empire was a monumental task worthy of being passed down for posterity. "Antiochus in many wars, though with difficulty and not in its entirety, restored his paternal kingdom." (Brill's New Jacoby, Memnon 434 F1 9.1)
