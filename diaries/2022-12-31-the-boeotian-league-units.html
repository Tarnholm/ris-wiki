The Boeotian League units

2022-12-31 · ahowl11

Hello again and Happy New Year's Eve (or New Year if you're on the other side of the world!) Today we have another DD for more of an obscure faction that not many people knew existed and it's not a faction that enjoyed independence for a long time. However, they did have an extensive military, were the first of the Greeks to make the leap from Hoplite to Phalangite, and they had a very fertile land. It's just unfortunate that they never did much. Today we give you the Boeotian League!

The Boeotian League is in a really tough situation as it's under the yoke of the Antigonids, in an uneasy alliance with Athens and very susceptible to attack by the Aetolians. However, they do have a nice roster to play with so, if you like a good challenge, make sure to play as the Boeotian League once 060 is released!
