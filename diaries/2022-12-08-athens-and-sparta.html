Athens and Sparta

2022-12-08 · ahowl11

Hello! Today the DD will be a two part DD, focusing on historic rivals who in our timeframe played much lesser roles in the politics of the era. 300 years ago, these two factions were amongst the most powerful and prestigious in the world as it was known. Now, in 270 BC, one struggles to even exist as a state and the other is having issues keeping up with the times. We present to you, Athens and Sparta!
