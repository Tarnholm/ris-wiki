As you can see Bithynia is a faction that blends units of Thracian style with Hellenic one giving you a fairly different playstyle.

That is all we have to show you for this time, but don't worry, we got plenty more to show ahead 0.6's release!

Just a few details that I want to pull out from the preview. 1) Unique bithynian helmets - there are two kinds that are attested to from various steles. First, there's one which comes to a point at the top and has a scrolled visor to it. Second there's a konos style helmet with crimped rim. You can see both on some of the variants of this Royal Peltast unit

2) Rimless Aspis shields - again well documented on various steles. We've chosen to show these decorated with various gods, goddeses - Athena (x2 - head only and one seated holding a winged Nike), Zeus and Apollo (blue eyes and golden hair) - and mythical creatures as well as one or two other symbols @Mausolos of Mylasa can probably add to the historical evidence on these equipment choices

3) Cavalry shields - again flattened aspis shields like some of the infantry. The patterns we've used are based on some motifs on Bithynian coins:

4) Bithynian Thureophoroi - relatively unarmoured compared with many of the thureophoroi units - their description is of having brown tunics with pale stripes

5) Thracians - we've shown some Thracian units before with the Antigonid preview, but this is the first time we've shown Thracian Peltasts - what many people think of when they think of Thracians - crescent pelte shield, phrygian caps and brightly patterned cloaks and tunics. Here they are in all their glory thanks to @AceSnake047 who did all the phrygian cap model variants

6) Bithynian Epibatai - another unique slinger marine unit - this time with thureos shields and pictures of Naiads on some of their shields

That's all for the moment
