Beliefs

← all cultures · all regions · all settlements · wiki index

RIS replaces the base game's handful of religions with 53 local beliefs — one per people, near enough — and spreads them across the map as a strength tier rather than a share. Every one has its own page: where it is, who its people are, who follows it, and what the mod does with it.

51 of the 53 are on the map at the campaign start, carried between them by 1,923 region tags across 1,312 regions. The other 2 — Anatolian and Greek — are declared but on no region: they are the umbrella entries the finer-grained beliefs sit under.

Where these answers come from, and the trap in reading the file

descr_beliefs.txt declares all 53, but not at the same indent: twelve of them, the Anatolian set, are written one tab deeper than the rest. A pattern anchored on a single tab finds 41 — and every one of the twelve it misses is a people the map uses, so the loss would have fallen exactly where it matters. A block is recognised here by the "religion icon" line it carries instead, which gives 53, and a flat pattern over the file text finds 53 of those lines and 53 "name" lines. All three numbers are printed on every run.

Where a belief is comes from the rel_<belief>_<tier> tags in descr_regions.txt — the digit is a 1-4 strength tier, not a percentage. Who follows it is stated by two files that do not always agree, and both are shown.

What a belief does, and what it does not

  • It is a number, not a key. No building level and no recruitment line in the mod is

conditioned on a belief. All 272 building level blocks and all 29,752 recruit lines were checked against every rel_<belief>_<tier> token: 0 levels and 0 recruit lines came back. Nothing is unlocked by what a province believes.

  • No belief creates unrest against another. All 53 declare a heretic multiplier of

0 and a heathen multiplier of 0. The base game's religious friction is switched off across the board.

  • What the tier does decide is how much religious_belief a settlement's own buildings

generate: the same level grants a different amount at each of the four tiers, and each page prints that mapping for its own belief.

  • Culture and belief are separate axes. 10 of the 22 cultures have factions on more than one belief. The cultures are on their own pages.

The 53 beliefs, by group

Majority and Minority are provinces: the ones where this is the largest belief, and the ones where it is held alongside a larger one. Together they are every province that holds it. Factions is not a count of provinces — it is how many factions have this as their state belief, so a faction with forty provinces counts once.

A group has no name of its own in the mod: descr_beliefs.txt declares a token and no text file localises it. 21 of the 22 group tokens are also belief tokens and take that belief's own declared name; the one that is not — hellenic — is printed as it stands, in lower case, rather than given a name this wiki made up.

Beliefs no faction defaults to

13 of the 53 are the default religion of no faction in descr_sm_factions.txt: Baltic, Anatolian, Mysian, Lydian, Pamphylian, Phrygian, Lycaonian, Isaurian, Cappadocian, Greek, Indo-Greek, Cypriot Greek, Pamphylian Greek. 11 of those are still on the map, held by provinces rather than by a state.
