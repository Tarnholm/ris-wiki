1,172 distinct units, against vanilla's 261. (The mod defines 1,697 entries; 525 of those are area-of-recruitment or horde variants of a unit already listed, merged here onto one page each.) 1,696 have a display name in the text files; 1,569 have a written description.

128 units still carry RIS's placeholder text ("this unit needs a description"). Those are shown as having no description rather than printing the placeholder, since text that looks like content but is not is worse than an honest gap.

Mercenaries

319 of these units are mercenaries — hired from a regional pool on the map, not recruited from a building. Every one of them is named "Mercenary …" here so a roster cannot be misread, which groups them together in the table below. 201 were already named that way by the mod; the prefix was added to the other 118.

How this wiki tells a mercenary from a regional unit

A mercenary is identified by its internal type beginning merc , not by the mercenary_unit attribute — that attribute is also on all 450 area-of-recruitment entries, so it labels 770 entries where only 320 are for hire.
