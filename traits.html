Character traits

← wiki index · all factions · all units

Characters in RTR: Imperium Surrectum are shaped by 3,873 visible traits — the virtues, vices, offices, wounds, habits and reputations that appear on a general's, governor's or agent's scroll. Behind them the file also declares 132 hidden bookkeeping traits the player never sees; they are counted here and excluded everywhere else.

How traits work

  • A trait is a ladder of levels. Points accumulate through triggers — battles fought,

turns idle, buildings raised — and each level shows once its points reach that level's threshold. The 3,873 visible traits carry 5,428 levels between them, fed by 14,376 triggers and 25,732 award rules.

  • Traits pull against each other. 2,342 of the visible traits name anti-traits: points in

one drain the other, so a character drifts toward drink or sobriety, courage or cowardice, never both.

  • 872 traits can pass from father to son at birth.
  • 1,069 trait levels grant an epithet — the "the Great" and "the Mad" a name carries.
  • Some traits are one-way: past a NoGoingBackLevel, the ladder no longer goes down.
