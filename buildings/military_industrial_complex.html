Barracks and Armoury

The heart of military excellence, crafting elite soldiers with the best Rome has to offer.

From these barracks stride Rome’s finest, clad in iron and ambition, ready to make their mark on history.

The Barracks and Armoury form the military core of the settlement, where seasoned soldiers are armed with the Republic’s finest weapons and armor. Skilled blacksmiths transform iron and bronze into shields, swords, and helmets worthy of the finest heavy infantry, archers, and cavalry. It is here that the Republic's elite warriors are readied, with the echo of hammer strikes and the hiss of cooling steel serving as a constant reminder of Rome's readiness for conquest.

Only the best emerge from these walls, ready to honour Rome with courage and steel.

Strategy

Unit recruitment requires more than just these barracks. To recruit units from your factional roster you either need a homeland government and these barracks OR a direct rule/indirect rule AND a colony OR the factional unit. While local units (AOR-Area of Recruitment, units with the diadem icon) often require less direct forms of government. See the Region Information Scroll for the AOR units that this region offers and hover over the units to see the requirements for recruitment.

Training Grounds

Where recruits learn to march, wield arms, and become the heart of the Republic’s army.

Here, the humble sons of Rome become the backbone of her empire, molded with discipline as their shield and unity as their sword.

The Training Grounds serve as the very beginning of a soldier’s journey into the ranks of the legion. Under the stern gaze of veteran centurions, the raw recruits learn to march as one, hold the line, and wield the essential tools of a legionary. Though the facility is humble, its purpose is noble: to ingrain unity, discipline, and the Roman way of warfare into each recruit.
