Dependency

Dependent polity or integrated ally

These lucky people are allies and friends of the Roman people. The title of Amicus is given to any allied state, but to be Socius et Amicus is to be bound with Rome in a treaty of mutual assistance. If they are in need Rome shall answer and if Rome calls upon them they shall support us to the best of their ability. Beyond that they manage their own affairs and maintain their own institutions and traditions. Many of the Socii are completely exempted from tribute, while others may have some additional obligations to Rome.

Full AOR recruiting: See Region Information Building for more details. No Factional recruitment. It increases the spread of the local cultures present within the region.

Wording varies by culture: 3 versions of this description exist in the game files.
