Copper Industry

← all buildings · wiki index

A single-level building: there is nothing to upgrade it into.

Copper Exports (Special exports)

Copper’s the secret sauce of wealth, trade it, use it, wear it.

The copper market is where wealth flows as freely as the molten metal itself.

Traders swap ingots, crafted goods, and, let’s be honest, a fair amount of gossip. Copper may not be the flashiest metal, but it’s the one that keeps everything ticking: from armor for your soldiers to the cups in your kitchen.

It’s a bustling hub of industry, commerce, and endless haggling for that extra shiny trinket.

Cost14,000 denariiBuild time10 turnsMinimum settlementcity

Requirements — all of these must hold before this level can be built.

  • any faction
  • Local Market at Local Market or above is built here
  • the region does not have the hidden resource nobuild (nobuilding)
  • the region has the copper resource
  • Direct Rule or Homeland
  • Mines (Industry) at Deep Mining Complex (Industry) or above is built here
  • no building tagged metals is built or queued here (no_other_metals)
  • anywhere in your empire does not have the hidden resource copper_supply_built (not_copper_supply_built)

What it does

  • +2 trade income
  • -3 public order (happiness)
  • -2 population growth
  • (Faction) Weaponsmiths gain +1 armour (does not stack with local copper, iron, flax, livestock)
  • (Faction) Metalworkers gain a trade bonus, this bonus is further increased if tin is also available (locally or supplied)
  • (Faction) Mints gain a tax bonus
