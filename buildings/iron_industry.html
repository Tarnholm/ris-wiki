Iron Industry

← all buildings · wiki index

A single-level building: there is nothing to upgrade it into.

Iron Exports (Special exports)

Here, metal can be poured, moulded and shaped into strength, wealth, and occasional cooking implements.

At the foundry, molten iron flows like fine wine at a victory feast, only it's less drinkable and more deadly.

Here, everything gets the iron treatment: from weapons that will cleave through shields to plows that will turn your fields into lunch.

This place is where iron gets beaten into submission, shaped into anything from armor to the occasional badly made frying pan. If it’s made of iron, it came from here.

Cost15,000 denariiBuild time10 turnsMinimum settlementcity

  • (Faction) Metalworkers gain a tax bonus (does not stack with local iron, lead or lead supply)
