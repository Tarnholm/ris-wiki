A single-level building: there is nothing to upgrade it into.

Pictures are the game's own building art. Where a culture ships none of its own, the game falls back to another culture's, and that is what is shown here.

Direct Rule

Direct Rule

Allows recruitment of tier 2 AOR units, and all factional units (requires colony level 1 outside unit's origin regions for units up to tier 3, and colony level 2 for top tier units). It increases spread of the local cultures present within the region in 50%.

Wording varies by culture: 4 versions of this description exist in the game files.

Cost2,500 denariiBuild time1 turnMinimum settlementtown
