Sulphur Mine (Industry)

Smells horrible, but very usefull.

Sulphur, the smelly rock of the gods. It stinks, it burns, and it can be a little too “mystical” for comfort.

Miners brave toxic fumes and volcanic lava (the worst job, really) to harvest this magical substance. And once they do, sulphur finds its way into everything from medicines, to fumigants to protect crops from pests. It is even used to ritually cleanse temples, meaning they would literally smell of brimstone.

Sulphur Fumigant Exports (Industry)

The game ships no picture of its own for this level and shows the Forum picture instead.

A site that supplies the empire with Sulphur for its crops.

This region is the sulphur supplier of the empire, making sure everyone who can afford it can benefit from its magic. On the great estates and on the farms of the more learned farmers the use of fumigants to protoct the crops from pests is rapidly catching on and experiments are being run on using sulphur to enrich the soil itself.

Meanwhile the miners here, many of them children and the enslaved, breathe poisonous fumes and dig into active volcanoes to make this all possible. Still, the land prospers, and that’s what matters, right?
