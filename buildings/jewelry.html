Jewellery Crafter (Industry)

The game ships no picture of its own for this level and shows the Local Barter picture instead.

Tinkering with valuable materials to make beautiful trinkets.

Deep in the earth, nature has hidden treasures that make emperors giddy and merchants rub their hands together in greedy anticipation. Even more so when they are turned into the fashion of status.

Whether precious metals or fiery ruby or mysterious emerald, they all promise untold wealth, power, and maybe even some magical protection (or at least a good excuse to flaunt them at dinner parties). These deposits are the starting point for the region's jewelry industry, where raw rocks are pulled from the earth and then handed over to artisans with a keen eye for turning them into glittering symbols of fortune and favor.

Jewellers provided the ancient world with one of the most potent methods of displaying wealth and status. The art of smithing precious metals was re-introduced to the western Mediterranean from the east, which also acted as the source for most of the raw ingredients.

The inevitable associations with far off places made jewellery all the more prestigious, as ownership signalled extensive trade connections. In time, the Greco-Roman world developed its own unique styles with ever increasing sophistication.

The cameo, a method of carving a layered gem with a raised relief of contrasting colour, is arguably the finest example, and was highly sought after at the time.

Artisan Jeweller (Industry)

The game ships no picture of its own for this level and shows the Local Market picture instead.

Turning out shiny things that will make anyone’s eyes water.

Once plucked from the earth, these humble stones ans shiny metals are ready to become the stars of the show.

Enter the gem cutters, goldsmiths, artists, and precision fanatics who can turn a dull rock into a shiny bauble worthy of the gods. These workshops hum with the sound of chisels and files as each gem is sliced and polished to perfection.

Whether it's a crown for the Basileus or a ring for his favorite champion, each gem is given the royal treatment, making the once ordinary stone as brilliant as its new owner’s ego. The jeweller will have even greater opportunities to create items of opulence if they can get their hands on exotic fine materials like ivory, amber, coloured glass or the gems of the distant east.

Jewellers Quarter (Industry)

The game ships no picture of its own for this level and shows the Forum picture instead.

Where gold and gems meet, and the rich get richer.

In the jeweller's hands, gold is more than a shiny metal or a coin to be traded about, it's the stuff of dreams (and sometimes nightmares if you don’t get your proportions right).

Here, gold and silver are meticulously crafted into more than just jewellry; they are symbols of status, power, and probably a little bit of divine favor.

Whether it's a ring that shows you're richer than your neighbor, or a brooch that says you're one of the wealthiest, this is where your nation’s elite comes to adorn themselves.

The jeweller isn’t just making baubles; they’re making statements.

These jewels might not make you immortal, but they will certainly make you look like you’re trying.

Whether it’s a flashy necklace or a crown fit for a conqueror, jewellry from this region is traded far and wide, ensuring the land’s prominence in the marketplace of luxury.
