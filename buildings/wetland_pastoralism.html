Seasonal Pasture Rotation (Adaptive husbandry)

The game ships no picture of its own for this level and shows the Farms+1 picture instead.

Seasonally rotating between grazing areas taking adavantage of natural resources along the way

The shepherds living in these vast wetlands have come to understand a great deal of its seasonal patterns. They see that it is best to not overgraze areas and to regularly move their flocks to other areas, with feeding on diverse foliage also producing healthier animals. Understanding the patterns of past flood events helps these people from going where they shouldn't and grazing their animals on risky wetland pastures, reducing losses to the herds.

Wetland pastoralism is available here because this is a wetlands region.

Note on the effects

Some levels of this building chain might have both positive and negative growth modifiers on the same building, this is due to a modding limitation. In these cases, all effects apply. So if it says +3 growth and -0.5 growth, your total growth is +2.5. Tip: put any building in the building queue (and only that one) and look at the 'Settlement income' and 'Settlement details' tabs to see its complete effects highlighted in green and red.

Reedland Pasturing (Adaptive husbandry)

The game ships no picture of its own for this level and shows the Farms picture instead.

Allowing livestock to graze freely within the wetlands

Most animals don't mind the muck. In fact, left to their own in a marshland they will do quite well feeding on the myriad nutritious plants, even going into mires that humans would find difficult to traverse. The challenges for the shepherds include keeping sight of their animals, to guard them from predators, save the unlucky animals that get stuck in the mires and occasionally having to move them out of the way of a flash flood.

Wetland pastoralism is available here because this is a wetlands region.

Managed Wetland Pastures (Adaptive husbandry)

The game ships no picture of its own for this level and shows the Farms+2 picture instead.

Effectively managing the marsh ecosystem for sustainable pastoralism

The peoples who live in, and with, the wetlands for generations may adapt to it to such an extent that all their social organization is based on its natural patterns. When to move the herds, when to harvest the reeds, and when to tactically move back to avoid drowning have become deeply honed instincts. And even true settlements can be supported from the wetlands when its potential is further enhanced by human management and its waterways made into avenues of trade. While the enemies of the marshland tribes find that subjugating a people so at home in such a hostile enviroment is hardly worth the trouble.

Wetland pastoralism is available here because this is a wetlands region.
