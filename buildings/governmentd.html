A single-level building: there is nothing to upgrade it into.

Pictures are the game's own building art. Where a culture ships none of its own, the game falls back to another culture's, and that is what is shown here.

Homeland

Homeland

Available only in designated homeland regions for each faction: Full factional recruitment, no AOR avaiability. It increases spread of the factional culture in 80%.

Wording varies by culture: 2 versions of this description exist in the game files.

Cost500 denariiBuild time1 turnMinimum settlementtown

What it does

  • extra construction points 1
  • recruits gain 1 experience
  • +10 taxable income
Show 1 effect that apply only in the circumstances shown
  • +1 population growth — only when not not_slave_supply_built
