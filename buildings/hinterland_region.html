Region Information Scroll

A comprehensive record of regional characteristics and available local troops based on government type.

This comprehensive administrative document catalogs all regional particularities, scripted mechanics and available Area of Recruitment units within the province.

Carefully maintained by your skilled scribes, the region information scroll is an essential reference for governors to understand local resources, special conditions, and military recruitment possibilities.

When your faction controls regions with local AOR units, these forces can be recruited as factional units regardless of the established government type.

However, access to pure AOR units varies according to administrative structure: Dependency status allows recruitment of all tiers including elite AOR units, Indirect Rule grants access up to tier 3 AOR units, Direct Rule permits up to tier 2 AOR units, while Homeland Government provides no access to local AOR forces, relying instead on standard factional units.
