Camel Herd (Rural)

A herd of humps, fit for desert duty and milk.

This sturdy herd of camels provides a lifeline to those navigating the arid lands, their ability to carry goods and endure sandy treks unmatched.

The herd is a priceless resource to traders and travelers alike, while also providing milk for those who prefer their drinks… chewy.

Cost1,000 denariiBuild time2 turnsMinimum settlementtownUpgrades toLarge Camel Herd (Rural)

Large Camel Herd (Rural)

Raising camels, the desert’s true champions and milk givers.

This large camel herd raises and breeds camels, essential for those traveling the unforgiving desert.

These resilient creatures offer transport, milk, and wool, ensuring the region’s trade routes stay well-stocked and secure.

A good camel from this farm means wealth and comfort for those who brave the dunes.
