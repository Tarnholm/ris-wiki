Tar Gatherer (Industry)

The game ships no picture of its own for this level and shows the Local Barter picture instead.

A site for gathering pitch.

At this remote site workers gather the mysterious and usefull substances that can make any surface waterproof, especially handy for ships, which you generally want to be watertight. In Mesopotamia and at the dead sea liquid naptha and bitumen naturally bubble up from depths unknown. While in the forests of the mediterranean and the north resinous woods like pine are gathered and burned in clay pits to produce tar. No matter the source the work is dirty, smelly and difficult.

Cost1,000 denariiBuild time2 turnsMinimum settlementtownUpgrades toPitch Rendering Kilns (Industry)

Requirements — all of these must hold before this level can be built.

  • The region has Pitch, Bitumen & Tars
  • Only one heavy industry building per settlement: no other may already be built or being built here
  • Government Building (Dependency, Indirect Rule, Direct Rule or Homeland)

What it does

  • +8 taxable income

Pitch Rendering Kilns (Industry)

The game ships no picture of its own for this level and shows the Local Market picture instead.

Pitch processing kilns further refine the gathered pitch.

The pitch that is gathered here was previously directly brought to town, but now that we have build up the kilns necessary to further refine the pitch we can create a more valuable product.

Pitch Exports (Industry)

The game ships no picture of its own for this level and shows the Forum picture instead.

A great industry supplying pitch to our factions shipyards.

All over the coasts great shipyards turn out and maintain numerous warships and ships of trade. But to do so they require massive amounts of pitch to keep our sailors from a watery grave. This region has developed the production capacity to meet this demand. Throughout the evening and the night the sky lights up from the fires of the rendering kilns.

Cost6,000 denariiBuild time6 turnsMinimum settlementcity

Requirements — all of these must hold before this level can be built.

  • Local Market or a higher level is built here
  • The region has Pitch, Bitumen & Tars
  • No Pitch Exports (Industry) is built anywhere in your empire
  • Direct Rule or Homeland

What it does

  • +12 taxable income
  • +2 trade income
  • -1 population growth
  • -1 public order (happiness)
  • (Faction) Ports gain +1 special attack for ships and slingers (does not stack with local pitch, but can stack with hemp and timber on higher level ports)
