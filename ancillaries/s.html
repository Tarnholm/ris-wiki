Satrapes tes Medias

never for 17 of the cultures.

Effects: +1 Law, +1 Influence

This man has been appointed as the Satrap of Media, which, after the death of Alexander, was Media divided into two parts: Media Magna (the land around Ecbatana and Rhagae) and Media Atropatene (Gazaca). Media was surrounded by Greek towns in pursuance of Alexander's plan to protect it from neighbouring barbarians, according to Polybius, and only Ekbatana retained its old character. Rhagai became the Greek town Europus; and with it Strabo names Laodicea, Apamea Heraclea and Achais. Most of them were founded by Seleucus I and his son Antiochus I, who settled a number of incoming Greek settlers in the newly established military colonies. Media, famous for its herds of prized Nisean horses, became an important manpower pool of katoikoi for the Seleucids, while also being of a strategic importance for its location on the eastern trade route to India.
