Priesthoods

← all retinue · all traits · wiki index

285 of the retinue are priests and priestesses — one per cult and city, from the Priest of Amun to the Priestess of Vesta. Each joins a character through temple and belief conditions and carries a small bonus; the in-game description is per-cult flavour text, read in game rather than reprinted here.
