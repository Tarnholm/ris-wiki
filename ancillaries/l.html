Labrys Maker

Effects: +1 Influence, +1 Training Units

This man is a mastersmith who produces the Labrys, the sacred double headed axe of Zeus. Zeus Labraundos at Mylasa, one of the two central deities of Caria, is named after the Labrys and believed to wield it in battle against the foes of Mylasa. Many Carian mercenaries, too, make use of the weapon, which has been sacred since the days of Minos on Crete.

Lacydes of Cyrene

unique — one in the world at a time · never alongside Philosopher, Orator.

Effects: +1 Management, +1 Influence

A Greek philosopher, was head of the Academy at Athens in succession to Arcesilaus, though the testimony of antiquity is that he adhered in general to the theory of Arcesilaus. He lectured in a garden called the Lacydeum and maintained the traditions of the Academy. He is a man of refined character, a hard worker and an accomplished orator.

No trigger in the file grants this — it comes from the campaign script or an event, and is listed here because the game still shows it when it arrives.
