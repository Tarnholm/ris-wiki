Urban Planner

never alongside Strategist, Tactician, Geomancer · never for 6 of the cultures.

Effects: -1 Squalor, +1 Health, +1 Unrest

Every good governor needs an administrator capable of reorganizing entire urban districts according to rational principles and population needs. Sure, some may have to leave their homes, but it’s all for the greater good, our vision of the greater good, at least.

Acquisition is tied to traits: Administrator, NaturalCharisma, NaturalIntelligence, Firm Personal Morality.
