This man is a gallos of Kybêlê, the great mother of all. Her cult is deeply connected with that of Attis: According to the poet Hermesianax, the born eunuch Attis, a young man, founded the cult of Kybêlê in Lydia before a boar slew him. In his honor, the Galloi castrate themselves and serve Kybêlêas as Attis did, though it has been reported that there are also some priestesses and uncastrated priests and followers of the great mother. The frenzy can overcome even the most reluctant, but many Greeks and Romans loathe the foreign, revolting practice. After the act is done, the followers of Kybêlê take the severed organ and run through the city, seizing women's clothing and adornment from whatever house they throw their organ into, before putting on these clothes and begging in the streets.

Gorgias - Discourses

never for 1 of the cultures.

Effects: +1 Influence, +1 Negotiation, -1 Unrest

Gorgias, called "the Nihilist," was a Greek sophist, pre-Socratic philosopher and rhetorician who was a native of Leontini in Sicily. Along with Protagoras, he forms the first generation of Sophists. Several doxographers report that he was a pupil of Empedocles, although he would only have been a few years younger. Gorgias ushered in rhetorical innovations involving structure and ornamentation and the introduction of paradoxologia – the idea of paradoxical thought and paradoxical expression. For these advancements, Gorgias has been labeled the ‘father of sophistry’. Gorgias is also known for contributing to the diffusion of the Attic dialect as the language of literary prose.

Acquisition is tied to the trait: Curious about Humanities.
