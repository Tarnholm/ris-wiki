Effects: +1 Influence

A public horse which belongs to the State, that is to the SPQR. During the times of the Kings of Rome, the practice of donating a fine horse to the citizen cavalry had begun and continued throughout the Republic. Many senators continued to be granted Public Horses after the splitting off of the Equites. To own a Public Horse was a sign of great importance, and to have one slain under you in battle even more so. Families would boast of how many Public Horses their ancestors had lost in battle.
