Cultures

← all factions · all settlements · beliefs · wiki index

Culture is the game's own grouping of its 239 factions, and it is not cosmetic. It settles what a settlement is drawn with, how far the mod lets it grow, which government levels can be installed there and a slice of what can be raised. 22 of them exist.

5 of the 22 carry a name the token would not have produced — barbarian is Gallic, carthaginian is Phoenician, eastern is Caucasian, w_hellenistic is Western Hellenistic, e_hellenistic is Eastern Hellenistic — so the token is never printed as a name here. Every name on these pages is the mod's own, from its text files.

Where these answers come from

descr_cultures.txt declares the 22 cultures, descr_sm_factions.txt assigns one to each of the 239 factions, and export_descr_buildings.txt conditions on them — not with a culture keyword, which appears in 0 requires expressions in the whole file, but through the engine's faction list, which takes a culture token as readily as a faction token. Of the 256 distinct tokens inside those lists, 233 are factions, one is all, and the remaining 22 are exactly the culture tokens.

The 22 cultures

Provinces is what the culture's factions hold at turn 0, out of the 1,305 settlements the campaign file places. Builds and units are what the mod gates on being of this culture. Belief spread is how many different default religion values its factions carry — culture and belief are separate axes, and this column is how separate.

What the files agree and disagree on

  • All 22 declare the same six-rung ladder with the same five population figures, and the

same unrest factors and agent costs. Those are not what tells one culture from another; the numbers are on the settlement size pages.

  • 8 of the 22 have a block in descr_sm_settlements.txt, which is the file that gives a

culture its strategy-map buildings and walls. The other 14 have none, and what their settlements are drawn with instead is not determined from the mod files.

  • 2 of those 8 stop short of the top rung. Gallic and Scythian declare nothing above City, while descr_cultures.txt gives all 22 cultures a max settlement level of Huge city. The two files contradict each other and this wiki does not resolve it — which one the engine obeys is not stated anywhere in the mod.
  • Culture does not fix belief. 13 of the 22 cultures have all their factions on one belief; the rest spread across up to 6. The beliefs are on their own pages.
