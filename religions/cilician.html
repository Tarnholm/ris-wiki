← all beliefs · all cultures · all regions · wiki index

Internal token cilician, in the Anatolian group. 11 of 1,312 regions · named as a people in 13 · 1 faction default to it

Where it is on the map

11 of the 1,312 regions carry a rel_cilician_… tag — 0.8% of the map. What matters is whether it is the majority belief in the settlement or a minority one. The file records a finer number behind that, and it is not a percentage — the one place it shows is how much religious_belief a building generates, which is the table further down.

What it gates

Nothing. No building level and no recruitment line in the mod is conditioned on a belief. A belief is a number a settlement carries, not a key that opens anything.

Unrest and identity

Every one of the 53 beliefs RIS declares sets both multipliers to 0. No belief in this mod creates unrest against another, whether they share a group or not — the religious tension of the base game is switched off across the board, and what the belief system is used for instead is the numbers above.

Its group

The Anatolian group (anatolian) holds 13 beliefs. The others are Anatolian, Cappadocian, Carian, Isaurian, Lycaonian, Lycian, Lydian, Mysian, Pamphylian, Paphlagonian, Phrygian, Pisidian.

A group has no name of its own in the mod — the token anatolian is all there is. The name above is the belief of that same token, which is the mod's own.
