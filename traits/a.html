Effects: no stat effects

This man has been adlected (recruited) into the Senate. At various points in Rome's history, certain characters of equestrian status were chosen to fill vacancies in the Senate, when membership fell below the ordained number of members. Julius Caesar, as dictator, and the triumvirs adlected a number of men into the Senate, but this practice became most commonplace under the Emperor Vespasian, who quickly needed to appoint a number of high-ranking officials after the loss of senatorial members during the reign of Nero and the subsequent civil war.
