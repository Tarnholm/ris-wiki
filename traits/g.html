Leader of Gymnasium: Honorary position involved with organizing and running the city’s gymnasia and palestrai. It keeps its citizens in fit shape, in times of peace, but it interferes with military training in times of war. Hence why Pyrrhos’ once given command of Taras and its army, shut down the City’s Gymnasia and Palestrai. It has been suggested, however, that this had more to do with keeping people out of crowded places, where conspiracies could form, rather than any actual interference from the Gymansia and Palestrai.\nThis man also has a "Veteran trainer" who is responsible for "Pezon taksies" (training infantry in marching) and for "Eutaksian" (proper battle arrangement of infantry men while excercising battle drills with mock weapons and their "thureoi" shields).
