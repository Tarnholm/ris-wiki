Native Grandson

Appears on family members — generals, governors, leaders · never on 13 of the cultures.

Effects: -1 Unrest

As the grandson of those warriors who once conquered this land, this man has no memory of the armed struggle that brought his family here, only the fields, festivals, and fiery debates of a land that is now is native country. While he still honours the traditions of his forebears, his voice now carries the accent of the locals, and his heart beats in time with the rhythms of the land he calls home.
