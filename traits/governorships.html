City governorships

← all traits · wiki index

One web of 1,419 mutually exclusive city titles — Archon, Shophet, Strategos and their kin — one per governable city. Holding the office grants the title and its bonus; losing the city takes it away. Every one of them excludes every other through the shared Not_Archon anti-trait, which is how the file keeps one governor to one city.

The traits

Triggers is how many rules in the file grant or remove the trait's points; a — means the campaign script or an office grants it directly. Each title's in-game description is its own per-city flavour text, read in game rather than reprinted 1,419 times here.
