What the game says at each level

Ugly, Ignorant Wife — This man’s wife isn’t exactly the centre of attention, and her grasp on intelligent conversation is as bad as her looks. Her most useful talent? Probably staying out of sight. Perhaps her father is a man of some influence, or perhaps she holds other merits, but whatever it is, her husband should probably keep her hidden behind closed doors, far away from social events.

Ugly Wife — This man’s wife won’t be winning any beauty contests, possibly due to some disease during childhood. Their children might need more than just good luck to avoid inheriting bad genes.

Ugly, Intelligent Wife — This man's wife may not be pretty nor healthy, but at least she is smart and fully devoted to her husband. Even from her sickbed, she may still be able to give him sound advice and benefit his career. She’s got the brains to make up for what the mirror doesn't find.

Adorable, Naive Wife — This man's wife may not be the smartest around, but she is charming and fertile and her husband is delighted with her. Who needs a house full of bookworms when you can raise a clan of mighty heroes?

Lovely Wife — This man's wife is healthy and their kids are the kind of strong, healthy offspring every family dreams of. Truly, this man is blessed with a wife who is an ornament to his household and his public image.

Charming, Intelligent Wife — This man’s wife is the whole package: beautiful, intelligent, and everyone adores her. She’s not just smart and charming, but also gives good advice. The kind of woman who makes her husband look good in every possible way.

Tedious Wife — Every time his wife speaks, you die a little. A wise man ignores the foolish advice of his wife, but not all men are wise...

Intelligent Wife — This man’s wife is sharp and always ready with good advice. A wise man knows to listen when she speaks, because her counsel could be the difference between success and disaster.

Retinue whose arrival is tied to this trait: Mistress.
