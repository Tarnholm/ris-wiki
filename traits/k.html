Appears on every character, agents included · sons inherit it 1% of the time · never on 12 of the cultures.

Effects: no stat effects · grants the epithet the Kainian

This man hails from the Kainoi, a tribe situated east of the river Ebros, in the territory of the Paitoi, between the Astai and the Korpiloi.
