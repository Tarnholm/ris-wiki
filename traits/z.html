Zakynthian

Appears on every character, agents included · sons inherit it 1% of the time · never on 17 of the cultures.

Effects: no stat effects · grants the epithet of Zakynthos

This man is from Zakynthos, conquered by Odysseus of Homeric fame before their voyage to Ilion. Zakynthos participated in the Peloponnesian War on the side of the Athenians before the local aristocrats formed a pro-Spartan government after the war.
