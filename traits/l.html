Legatus Augusti Arabiae Petraeae — This man is the Imperial Legate of Arabia Petraea, a frontier province of the Roman Empire. It was annexed by Trajan, like many other eastern frontier provinces of the Roman Empire, but held onto unlike Armenia, Mesopotamia and Assyria, well after Trajan's rule - its desert frontier being called the Limes Arabicus.\nAs a frontier province it included a desert populated by the nomadic Saraceni and bordered the Parthian hinterland. Though subject to eventual attack and deprivation by the Parthians and Palmyrenes, it had nothing like the constant incursions faced in other areas on the Roman frontier, such as Germany and North Africa, nor the entrenched cultural presence that defined the other, more Hellenized, eastern provinces.\nBefore Roman control, the area was ruled by Rabbel II, last king of the Nabataeans until 106 AD.
