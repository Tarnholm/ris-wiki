Quaestor

Appears on family members — generals, governors, leaders · pulls against Former Quaestor, Restore Quaestor, Imperator Caesar · at most 12 characters may hold it · never on 21 of the cultures.

Effects: +1 Influence

As the first step on the Cursus Honorum, this man has succeeded in becoming a Quaestor, an elected magistrates whose primary function is to supervise the public treasury of Rome. He also looks after the financial affairs of the military, and can be assigned to generals or provincial governors.
