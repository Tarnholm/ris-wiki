Selgian Military Reforms

Who gets it: Selge

Esteemed council of Selge, today is a glorious day. Our officers have worked hard to make this day reality, and now here, in front of you, stand our first riders who can fight with a Hoplite shield from horseback. Selected as Ephebes when they were still young, they are now some of Pisidia's most renowned warriors, and they will terribly strike the enemy from their mounts. Protected by the great Greek round shield, they can deflect any missile the enemy can come up with. Selge is glorious!

How to get it

It fires when at least 20 battles have been fought by Selge, led by a general.

The game checks this at the end of every round.

What it unlocks

1 unit can be recruited once it fires:
