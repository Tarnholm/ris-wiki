The Knossian Treaty

Who gets it: Aetolian League

Priest of Apollo: Assembly of Aitolia, welcome the envoy of Knossos!

Aitolians: Hooray!

Knossian envoy: <bows> The people and the council of the Knossians thanks you, men of Aitolia, for your friendship and support. We have come to pay our debts. These young men here are some of our most talented up-and-coming young archers. They will join your ranks and train your Ephebes how to use the bow like Apollo himself!

Aitolians: Hooray! Hooray! Long live Knossos!

How to get it

It fires when a settlement held by Aetolian League lies in the Cretan recruitment area.

The game checks this at the end of every round.

What it unlocks

1 unit can be recruited once it fires:
