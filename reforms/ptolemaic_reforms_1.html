Mobilization of the Machimoi

Who gets it: Ptolemaic Empire, Ptolemaic Rebels and Egyptians

Hail, pharaoh! Macedonian and Greek manpower is hard to come by, and the everlasting war against the Seleucids and our own rebellious subjects is draining our small pool of true Hellenes... Fortunately, the local Egyptian population is swelling, and they are eager to fight! If we provide them with a small plot of land to sustain themselves, and train them in our superior Macedonian tradition, we can draft thousands of these ‘Machimoi’ (warriors) into our army. These men are willing to die for you, and for the Ptolemaic Empire. The bravest might even form ‘Epilektoi’ regiments and could guard our court! Beware, though, that they do not get too powerful, as rumours of the local’s strive for independence have been snuffed out within the ranks of these new recruits…

How to get it

It fires when any one of these holds:

  • a settlement held by Ptolemaic Empire is Memphis and it is a huge city or larger
  • it is turn 202 (220 BC) or later

The game checks this at the end of every round.

Opens the way to: Amnesty & Restructuring of the Machimoi, The Great Egyptian Revolt.
