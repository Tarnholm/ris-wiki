Bosporan Thureos Reform

Who gets it: Bosporans

Hail, Archon of Pantikapaion and Phanagoreia and Basileios of the Kimmerian Bosporos! A military innovation has occurred in your realm. Our horsemen have tested the oval Celtic shield, and they are pleased with the results. You may now recruit new cavalry units equipped with the Thureos from the royal barracks. May Herakles lead us to victory!

How to get it

It fires when a settlement held by Bosporans is a huge city or larger.

The game checks this at the end of every round.

What it unlocks

1 unit can be recruited once it fires:
