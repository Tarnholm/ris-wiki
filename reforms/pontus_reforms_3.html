Mithridatic Romanized Reforms

Who gets it: Pontus

Chaire, mighty Basileios, ruler of the land, mightiest of all the men in mother Asia! By Kybele and Athena, your greatness knows no bounds. The poets are already writing verses about your great victory against the western Barbarians. At the same time, our best engineers and officers have worked hard to reproduce the shiny armour of the Romaioi you spoke about. New troops that are equipped exactly like our foes are now available for recruitment and will ram home our advantage: Pontic courage!

What it unlocks

1 unit can be recruited once it fires:

UnitWho can raise it
Pontic Imitation LegionariesPontus

How to get it

It fires when at least 5 battles have been fought by Pontus, against Roman armies.

The game checks this at the end of every round.
