Pontic Hellenistic Reforms

Who gets it: Pontus

Chaire, Basileios! Let Zeus and Mithras attest how splendidly you have led our kingdom, how great our wealth has become and how strong our army. We have followed your latest instructions and implemented the training of the terrible Macedonian phalanx, which has hurt us so profoundly in the past. No longer will Macedonian pikes defeat Pontic bravery, for now both former slaves and men born free will be able to fight back as phalangites themselves. Additionally, a new royal guard has been formed, which will make you proud: it is the Hypaspists of the demigod Alexander reborn! Cappadocia, Paphlagonia and the friendly Sea be praised!

How to get it

It fires when at least 25 settlements are held by Pontus.

The game checks this at the end of every round.
