Adoption of the Thorakitai

Who gets it: Seleucid Empire, Seleucid Rebels (seleucid_rebels), Seleucid Rebels (seleucid_rebels2) and Lysiad Kingdom

Greetings, mighty Basileios, may you live long and prosper, Agathe Tyche. Our Macedonian soldiers are the best in the world, sons of the divine Herakles themselves, worthy of Alexander the demigod. Yet, sometimes us mortals have to change, and it seems that now a new type of armour has become available, which will protect the Thorax (chest) of our soldiers. Allow us to retrain our men to fight as Thorakitai and you will see how effective our Greek armour is. Long live the dynasty of Seleukos!

How to get it

It fires when at least 15 mercenary units have been hired by Seleucid Empire.

The game checks this at the end of every round.

What it unlocks
