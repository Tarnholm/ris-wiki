Greek Reforms: Thorakitai

Who gets it: Heraclea Pontica, Histria and Olbia

Chairete, noble Greeks! I speak to you today in the hope of instituting much-needed reforms in our military. In this age of barbarism and endless war, brave citizens wielding aspis and spear no longer suffice to stem the tide of ruin. Gauls threaten the Greek cities of Asia, Roman barbarians already rule our brothers in the West, and Hellas itself is under the heel of Makedonian kings. We could be next. If our fair city is to survive, we MUST adapt!

Assembly: Yes yes, get on with it already! What do you suggest?

I propose we raise the necessary funds and arm professional regiments of men in the style of the very barbarian brutes who threaten us.

Assembly: <murmuring>

With a small investment, we could equip and train our men as thorakitai. Give our men thureos shields, swords, javelins, and sturdy armour, and I guarantee you we will be able to match any foe!

Assembly: <Roars of agreement>

How to get it

It fires when it is turn 50 (258 BC) or later.

The game checks this at the end of every round.

What it unlocks

1 unit can be recruited once it fires:
