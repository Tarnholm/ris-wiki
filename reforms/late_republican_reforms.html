The Late Republican Reforms

Who gets it: Rome, Roman Rebels (roman_rebels_1), Roman Rebels (roman_rebels_2) and The Roman Senate — once it fires it applies to all of them at once

The continuous wars of our Republic have shown the limits of our levy system and stretched it to its breaking point. The traditional property requirements for military service have left us with a severe manpower crisis, unable to call upon sufficient man to enlist in the various armies needed to protect Rome's borders.

In response, extensive military reforms have been enacted on the recruitment, equipment, deployment and training of the Roman legions. Despite the opposition of some, the property qualifications have been abolished, allowing the landless masses, the capite censi, to enlist. The state now provides their arms and armour, standardising their equipment. The lines of Hastati, Principes, and Triarii have been replaced by a unified formation: the Cohort.

Rome can now put in the field professional, full-time standing armies. However, a dangerous precedent has been set. Recruited from the impoverished masses, these men rely on their commanders for their pay, plunder, and eventual land to settle after service. Their loyalty is shifting away from the Senate, and toward these ambitious generals who lead them.

Who will be able to stop them if their commander decides to cross the Rubicon and march against Rome?

How to get it

It fires when the conditions below are met.

For 2 turns, counted at the start of each turn, all of these hold:

  • italic_revolt_done has been set — checked at the end of a faction's turn, all of these hold:
  • it is turn 120 (241 BC) or later
  • The Gracchan Reforms has happened
  • at least 20 of these buildings stand in settlements that lie in the Camillan recruitment area: Small Colony or Large Colony
  • a 35% chance each time it is checked
  • the faction Italics has been destroyed
The script, step by step

italic_cw1_gate is kept by the campaign script. It changes:

  • At the start of each turn, if italic_revolt_done = 1 and the faction Italics has been destroyed: goes up by 1

The game checks this at the end of every round.

Comes after: The Gracchan Reforms, The Italic Revolt.
