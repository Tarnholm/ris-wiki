Polybian Reform

Who gets it: Rome, Roman Rebels (roman_rebels_1), Roman Rebels (roman_rebels_2) and The Roman Senate — once it fires it applies to all of them at once

Hail, Consul! Decades of brutal warfare against Samnites, Gauls, and Carthaginians have shown the need for further military innovations. In response to these challenges, our armies are now being armed with the large oval scutum, the armor-piercing pilum, and the Iberian gladius. Our legions stand ready to defend the Republic and secure Roman hegemony across the Mediterranean.

How to get it

It fires when any one of these holds:

  • you are playing Rome, Roman Rebels (roman_rebels_1), Roman Rebels (roman_rebels_2) or The Roman Senate and at least 71 settlements are held by Rome, Roman Rebels (roman_rebels_1), Roman Rebels (roman_rebels_2) and The Roman Senate between them
  • it is turn 72 (253 BC) or later, you are playing none of Rome, Roman Rebels (roman_rebels_1), Roman Rebels (roman_rebels_2) and The Roman Senate
  • fewer than 15 settlements are held by Rome, Roman Rebels (roman_rebels_1), Roman Rebels (roman_rebels_2) and The Roman Senate between them, you are playing none of Rome, Roman Rebels (roman_rebels_1), Roman Rebels (roman_rebels_2) and The Roman Senate

The game checks this at the end of every round.

Opens the way to: The Gracchan Reforms.
