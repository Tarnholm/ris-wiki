The Gracchan Reforms

Who gets it: Rome, Roman Rebels (roman_rebels_1), Roman Rebels (roman_rebels_2) and The Roman Senate

Tiberius Gracchus, as Tribune of the Plebs sought to heal the growing divide in the Republic by passing the Lex Agraria. This land reform aimed to confiscate excess public lands hoarded by the elite and redistribute them to Rome's landless citizens and impoverished veterans.

By bypassing the Senate and appealing directly to the masses, Gracchus championed the cause of the Populares. However, he drew the ire of the Optimates, who saw their wealth, vast estates, and traditional constitutional authority directly threatened by him. Led by conservative senators, a mob attacked Gracchus and his supporters on the Capitoline Hill. The Tribune was beaten to death, and his body was thrown into the Tiber River.

This assassination marked a dark turning point in Roman history. The sacred immunity of the tribunate has been violated, opening the door to a terrifying era of political violence in Rome.

Tiberius land reforms survived his death; family allies, including his younger brother Gaius, took places on the land commission set up by the law and distributed land over the next few years. A decade later, Gaius too became Tribune of the Plebs and proposed much more wide-ranging reforms, such as the famous 'Bread Dole', subject of popular acclaim, which also led to his death.

How to get it

It fires when the campaign counter gracchi_event_done is exactly 1 (see below).

gracchi_event_done is kept by the campaign script. It changes:

  • At the start of each turn, if gracchi_event_done = 0, it is turn 100 (246 BC) or later, a 35% roll succeeds, Polybian Reform has happened and gracchi_farms >= 5: is set to 1
  • gracchi_farms — never rises, so any test that needs it above 0 fails:
  • Recounted over every settlement of roman — no faction is called roman, so this counts nothing: 1 for each settlement with Irrigated Great Estates (Crops), Great Estates (Crops), Livestock Estates (Husbandry), Irrigation Aqueducts (Crops), Crop Rotation (Crops) or Great Droveways (Husbandry)

The game checks this at the end of every round.

Comes after: Polybian Reform.

What it unlocks

No recruitment line tests this reform and it converts no units: it changes nothing a player can recruit.
