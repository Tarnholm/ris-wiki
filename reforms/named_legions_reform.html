Named Legions are now available

Who gets it: Rome, Roman Rebels (roman_rebels_1), Roman Rebels (roman_rebels_2) and The Roman Senate — once it fires it applies to all of them at once

Hooray

How to get it

It fires when the campaign counter cw2_wait is at least 25 (see below) and a 35% roll succeeds.

cw2_wait (“turns elapsed since war one resolved”) is kept by the campaign script. It changes:

  • At the start of each turn, if you are playing Rome, cw_enabled = 1 (“0 turns the entire CW off”), cw1_done = 1 (“1 once the split has happened”), cw1_resolved = 1 (“1 once war one's revolt has actually landed”), cw1_landed = 1 (“1 once war one's provinces ACTUALLY changed hands”), cw2_armed = 0 (“1 once the conditions are met”), cw2_wait_done = 0 (“1 once the fuse has burned down”) and exactly 1 settlement is held by Roman Rebels (roman_rebels_1): goes up by 1
  • At the start of each turn, if you are playing Roman Rebels (roman_rebels_1), cw_enabled = 1 (“0 turns the entire CW off”), cw1_done = 1 (“1 once the split has happened”), cw1_resolved = 1 (“1 once war one's revolt has actually landed”), cw1_landed = 1 (“1 once war one's provinces ACTUALLY changed hands”), cw2_armed = 0 (“1 once the conditions are met”), cw2_wait_done = 0 (“1 once the fuse has burned down”) and exactly 1 settlement is held by Rome: goes up by 1
  • At the start of each turn, if cw_enabled = 1 (“0 turns the entire CW off”), cw1_done = 1 (“1 once the split has happened”), cw1_resolved = 1 (“1 once war one's revolt has actually landed”), cw1_landed = 1 (“1 once war one's provinces ACTUALLY changed hands”), cw2_armed = 0 (“1 once the conditions are met”), cw2_wait_done = 0 (“1 once the fuse has burned down”) and cw2_is_local_roman = 0 (“1 when the player is julii or rebels_1”): goes up by 1

The game checks this at the end of every round.

What it unlocks

No recruitment line tests this reform and it converts no units: it changes nothing a player can recruit.
