Ptolemaic Military Reforms

Who gets it: Ptolemaic Empire and Ptolemaic Rebels

Hail, pharaoh! Following the reforms of Polykrates, our Therapeia guard cavalry has been retrained to become more efficient in battle. They have also been re-equipped with sturdy bronze muscle cuirasses and plumed helmets, a wonderful sight to behold. Inspired by the Cretans, Thracians and Gauls arriving in Polykrates' wake, part of our men have been trained in their fighting style as Neo-Cretans and Thorakitai. Combining the best of old and new, our reinvigorated forces will surely go on to dominate all of Alexanders' conquests and much beyond!

How to get it

It fires when at least 10 units of Ptolemaic General's Bodyguard have been recruited by Ptolemaic Empire.

The game checks this at the end of every round.
