Dardanian Pikemen Reform

Who gets it: Dardania

DARDANIAN SOLDIERS! STAND AT ATTENTION! Hold you pike straight up! STRAIGHT UP I SAID! Keep a close eye on what the man in front of you does! NOW, MARCH! Good … and now … stop! STOP, I SAID, YOU BUFFOONS! Back into you positions! … Good. Now, resume marching! Aaaaand wheel to the right! … WHAT ARE YOU DOING! CAN’T YOU FOOLS TELL LEFT FROM RIGHT? We’ll repeat that until you’ve learned to properly march in formation and wheel the phalanx around! If we want to take on our damned Macedonian neighbours, we must meet them on equal ground! And from the start: MEN! READY YOUR PIKES! STAND AT ATTENTION! NOW MARCH!

How to get it

It fires when at least 10 battles have been fought by Dardania, led by a general and against Antigonid Kingdom and Bylazora, Skoupi and Stoboi are all held by Dardania.

The game checks this at the end of every round.

What it unlocks

1 unit can be recruited once it fires:
