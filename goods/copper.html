What it does

Where these answers come from, and what an unestablished effect means

What a good does is read off the clauses the mod conditions on it — a requirement on a building level, on a numeric effect, or on a recruitment line, including the ones reached through the building file's own aliases. Negated clauses count: withholding something is an effect. Where nothing in the mod conditions on a good, the page says no effect established rather than describing what the word suggests.

The mod calls this good copper internally; that is the word the conditions on this page are written against.

  • Lets you build — 9 building levels: Blacksmith (Industry), Armourer (Industry), Foundry (Industry), Toolmakers' Workshop (Industry), Metalwork Artisans (Industry), Metalworking District (Industry), Mines (Industry), Deep Mining Complex (Industry), Copper Exports (Special exports)
  • Numeric effects where it is present — armour upgrade +1 (Blacksmith (Industry)); armour upgrade +1 (Armourer (Industry)); armour upgrade +1 (Foundry (Industry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Toolmakers' Workshop (Industry)); trade income +2 to +3 (Metalwork Artisans (Industry)); trade income +3 to +4 (Metalworking District (Industry)); taxable income +10 (Local Mint (Civic)); taxable income +14 (State Mint (Civic)); taxable income +20 (Great Mint (Civic))
  • Numeric effects it withholds — these are granted only where it is absent: trade income +1 (Toolmakers' Workshop (Industry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Metalwork Artisans (Industry)); trade income +2 to +3 (Metalworking District (Industry)); taxable income +6 (Local Mint (Civic)); taxable income +10 to +14 (State Mint (Civic)); taxable income +14 to +20 (Great Mint (Civic))
