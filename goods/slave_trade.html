What it does

Where these answers come from, and what an unestablished effect means

What a good does is read off the clauses the mod conditions on it — a requirement on a building level, on a numeric effect, or on a recruitment line, including the ones reached through the building file's own aliases. Negated clauses count: withholding something is an effect. Where nothing in the mod conditions on a good, the page says no effect established rather than describing what the word suggests.

The mod calls this good slave_trade internally; that is the word the conditions on this page are written against.

  • Lets you build — 3 building levels: Slave Trader (Urban), Slave Auction (Urban), Slave Trading Hub (Urban)
  • Numeric effects where it is present — taxable income +5 (Irrigation Aqueducts (Crops)); taxable income +10 (Irrigated Great Estates (Crops)); taxable income +5 (Crop Rotation (Crops)); taxable income +10 (Great Estates (Crops)); taxable income +5 (Lowland Deforestation (Crops)); taxable income +10 (Reclaimed Woodland Estates (Crops)); taxable income +5 (Subterranean Aquaducts (Crops)); taxable income +5 (River Diversion Dyke (Crops)); taxable income +10 (Reclaimed land Estates (Crops)); taxable income +5 (Saltworks (Industry)); taxable income +8 (Salt Refiner (Industry)); taxable income +10 (Salt Exports (Industry)); taxable income +3 (Sulphur Mine (Industry)); taxable income +8 (Extensive Sulphur Mine (Industry)); taxable income +10 (Sulphur Fumigant Exports (Industry)); taxable income +3 (Marble Pit (Industry)); taxable income +6 (Marble Quarry (Industry)); taxable income +10 (Marble Exports (Industry)); taxable income +4 (Stone Quarry (Industry)); taxable income +8 (Stone Cutter (Industry)); taxable income +10 (Stone Exports (Industry)); taxable income +4 (Mines (Industry)); taxable income +8 (Deep Mining Complex (Industry)); public order (happiness) +2 (Arena (Leisure)); public order (happiness) +5 to +6 (Amphitheatre (Leisure)); public order (happiness) +7 to +9 (Colosseum (Leisure))
