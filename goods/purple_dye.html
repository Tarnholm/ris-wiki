What it does

Where these answers come from, and what an unestablished effect means

What a good does is read off the clauses the mod conditions on it — a requirement on a building level, on a numeric effect, or on a recruitment line, including the ones reached through the building file's own aliases. Negated clauses count: withholding something is an effect. Where nothing in the mod conditions on a good, the page says no effect established rather than describing what the word suggests.

The mod calls this good purple_dye internally; that is the word the conditions on this page are written against.

  • Lets you build — 4 building levels: Murex Snail Collectors (Industry), Purple Dye Works (Industry), Purple Dye Factory (Industry), Purple Dye Exports (Industry)
  • Numeric effects where it is present — trade income +3 (Textile Factory (Urban)); trade income +3 to +4 (Luxury Clothiers Quarter(Urban))
  • Numeric effects it withholds — these are granted only where it is absent: trade income +2 (Textile Factory (Urban)); trade income +2 to +3 (Luxury Clothiers Quarter(Urban))
