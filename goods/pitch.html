What it does

Where these answers come from, and what an unestablished effect means

What a good does is read off the clauses the mod conditions on it — a requirement on a building level, on a numeric effect, or on a recruitment line, including the ones reached through the building file's own aliases. Negated clauses count: withholding something is an effect. Where nothing in the mod conditions on a good, the page says no effect established rather than describing what the word suggests.

The mod calls this good pitch internally; that is the word the conditions on this page are written against.

  • Lets you build — 3 building levels: Tar Gatherer (Industry), Pitch Rendering Kilns (Industry), Pitch Exports (Industry)
  • Numeric effects where it is present — taxable income -5 (Trade Port); other weapon upgrade +1 (Trade Port); taxable income -8 (Shipwright); other weapon upgrade +1 to +2 (Shipwright); taxable income -12 (Dockyard); other weapon upgrade +1 to +3 (Dockyard); population health +3 (Physician's Clinics (Healing)); population health +4 to +5 (Healing Sanctuary (Healing))
  • Numeric effects it withholds — these are granted only where it is absent: taxable income -10 to -5 (Trade Port); taxable income -15 to -8 (Shipwright); other weapon upgrade +1 to +2 (Shipwright); taxable income -20 to -12 (Dockyard); other weapon upgrade +1 to +2 (Dockyard); population health +2 (Physician's Clinics (Healing)); population health +3 to +4 (Healing Sanctuary (Healing))
