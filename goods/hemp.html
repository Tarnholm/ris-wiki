What it does

Where these answers come from, and what an unestablished effect means

What a good does is read off the clauses the mod conditions on it — a requirement on a building level, on a numeric effect, or on a recruitment line, including the ones reached through the building file's own aliases. Negated clauses count: withholding something is an effect. Where nothing in the mod conditions on a good, the page says no effect established rather than describing what the word suggests.

The mod calls this good hemp internally; that is the word the conditions on this page are written against.

  • Lets you build — 3 building levels: Hemp Farm (Rural), Hemp Plantation and Ropemaker (Rural), Cordage Exports (Rural)
  • Numeric effects where it is present — trade income +1 (Slash and Burn (Crops)); trade income +1 (Assart Clearings (Crops)); trade income +1 (Rotational Grove Fields (Crops)); trade income +1 (Lowland Deforestation (Crops)); trade income +2 (Reclaimed Woodland Estates (Crops)); trade income +1 (Qanat Well (Crops)); trade income +1 (Qanat Network (Crops)); trade income +2 (Subterranean Aquaducts (Crops)); trade income +1 (Marsh Drainage Channels (Crops)); trade income +1 (Reclamation Embankments (Crops)); trade income +1 (Drainage Pumps (Crops)); trade income +1 (River Diversion Dyke (Crops)); trade income +2 (Reclaimed land Estates (Crops)); taxable income -5 (Trade Port); other weapon upgrade +1 (Trade Port); taxable income -8 (Shipwright); other weapon upgrade +1 to +2 (Shipwright); taxable income -12 (Dockyard); other weapon upgrade +1 to +3 (Dockyard)
  • Numeric effects it withholds — these are granted only where it is absent: taxable income -10 to -5 (Trade Port); taxable income -15 to -8 (Shipwright); other weapon upgrade +1 to +2 (Shipwright); taxable income -20 to -12 (Dockyard); other weapon upgrade +1 to +2 (Dockyard)
