What it is

What it does

Where these answers come from, and what an unestablished effect means

What a good does is read off the clauses the mod conditions on it — a requirement on a building level, on a numeric effect, or on a recruitment line, including the ones reached through the building file's own aliases. Negated clauses count: withholding something is an effect. Where nothing in the mod conditions on a good, the page says no effect established rather than describing what the word suggests.

The mod calls this good salt internally; that is the word the conditions on this page are written against.

  • Lets you build — 4 building levels: Garum Factory (Urban), Saltworks (Industry), Salt Refiner (Industry), Salt Exports (Industry)
  • Numeric effects where it is present — trade income +1 to +2 (Enclosures (Husbandry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Breeding Farms (Husbandry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Great Droveways (Husbandry)); trade income +1 to +3 (Livestock Estates (Husbandry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Highland Pasturing (Husbandry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Transhumance (Husbandry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Great Transhumance Roads (Husbandry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Water Covenants (Husbandry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Breeding Stock Exchanges (Husbandry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Forest Gardening (Adaptive husbandry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Managed Woodland Pastures (Adaptive husbandry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Seasonal Pasture Rotation (Adaptive husbandry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Managed Wetland Pastures (Adaptive husbandry)); trade income +1 (Fish Salter (Urban)); trade income +2 (Salted Fish Market (Urban))
  • Numeric effects it withholds — these are granted only where it is absent: trade income +1 (Enclosures (Husbandry)); trade income +1 (Breeding Farms (Husbandry)); trade income +1 (Great Droveways (Husbandry)); trade income +2 (Livestock Estates (Husbandry)); trade income +1 (Highland Pasturing (Husbandry)); trade income +1 (Transhumance (Husbandry)); trade income +1 (Great Transhumance Roads (Husbandry)); trade income +1 (Water Covenants (Husbandry)); trade income +1 (Breeding Stock Exchanges (Husbandry)); trade income +1 (Forest Gardening (Adaptive husbandry)); trade income +1 (Managed Woodland Pastures (Adaptive husbandry)); trade income +1 (Seasonal Pasture Rotation (Adaptive husbandry)); trade income +1 (Managed Wetland Pastures (Adaptive husbandry)); trade income +1 (Salted Fish Market (Urban))
