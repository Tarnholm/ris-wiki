What it does

Where these answers come from, and what an unestablished effect means

What a good does is read off the clauses the mod conditions on it — a requirement on a building level, on a numeric effect, or on a recruitment line, including the ones reached through the building file's own aliases. Negated clauses count: withholding something is an effect. Where nothing in the mod conditions on a good, the page says no effect established rather than describing what the word suggests.

The mod calls this good coal internally; that is the word the conditions on this page are written against.

  • Lets you build — 5 building levels: Blacksmith (Industry), Armourer (Industry), Foundry (Industry), Mines (Industry), Deep Mining Complex (Industry)
  • Numeric effects where it is present — simple weapon upgrade +1 (Blacksmith (Industry)); bladed weapon upgrade +1 (Blacksmith (Industry)); missile weapon upgrade +1 (Blacksmith (Industry)); simple weapon upgrade +1 (Armourer (Industry)); bladed weapon upgrade +1 (Armourer (Industry)); missile weapon upgrade +1 (Armourer (Industry)); simple weapon upgrade +1 to +2 (Foundry (Industry)); bladed weapon upgrade +1 to +2 (Foundry (Industry)); missile weapon upgrade +1 (Foundry (Industry))
  • Numeric effects it withholds — these are granted only where it is absent: simple weapon upgrade +1 (Foundry (Industry)); bladed weapon upgrade +1 (Foundry (Industry))
