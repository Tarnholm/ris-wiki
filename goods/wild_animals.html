What it does

Where these answers come from, and what an unestablished effect means

What a good does is read off the clauses the mod conditions on it — a requirement on a building level, on a numeric effect, or on a recruitment line, including the ones reached through the building file's own aliases. Negated clauses count: withholding something is an effect. Where nothing in the mod conditions on a good, the page says no effect established rather than describing what the word suggests.

The mod calls this good wild_animals internally; that is the word the conditions on this page are written against.

  • Lets you build — 3 building levels: Hunting Grounds (Rural), Fur Market (Rural), Wild Animals Supply (Rural)
  • Numeric effects where it is present — trade income +1 (Nomad Communal Herding (Husbandry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Water Covenants (Husbandry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Breeding Stock Exchanges (Husbandry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Warlord's Reserve Herd (Husbandry)); trade income +1 (Mast Pasturing (Adaptive husbandry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Forest Gardening (Adaptive husbandry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Managed Woodland Pastures (Adaptive husbandry)); trade income +1 (Reedland Pasturing (Adaptive husbandry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Seasonal Pasture Rotation (Adaptive husbandry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Managed Wetland Pastures (Adaptive husbandry)); public order (happiness) +4 to +6 (Amphitheatre (Leisure)); public order (happiness) +6 to +9 (Colosseum (Leisure))
  • Numeric effects it withholds — these are granted only where it is absent: trade income +1 (Water Covenants (Husbandry)); trade income +1 (Breeding Stock Exchanges (Husbandry)); trade income +1 (Warlord's Reserve Herd (Husbandry)); trade income +1 (Forest Gardening (Adaptive husbandry)); trade income +1 (Managed Woodland Pastures (Adaptive husbandry)); trade income +1 (Seasonal Pasture Rotation (Adaptive husbandry)); trade income +1 (Managed Wetland Pastures (Adaptive husbandry)); public order (happiness) +3 to +5 (Amphitheatre (Leisure)); public order (happiness) +4 to +7 (Colosseum (Leisure))
