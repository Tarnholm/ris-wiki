What it does

Where these answers come from, and what an unestablished effect means

What a good does is read off the clauses the mod conditions on it — a requirement on a building level, on a numeric effect, or on a recruitment line, including the ones reached through the building file's own aliases. Negated clauses count: withholding something is an effect. Where nothing in the mod conditions on a good, the page says no effect established rather than describing what the word suggests.

The mod calls this good elephants internally; that is the word the conditions on this page are written against.

  • Lets you build — 3 building levels: Elephant Hunters (Rural), Ivory Workshop (Rural), Ivory Trader (Rural)
  • Numeric effects where it is present — farming level +3 (Assart Clearings (Crops)); farming level +4 (Rotational Grove Fields (Crops)); farming level +5 to +6 (Lowland Deforestation (Crops)); farming level +7 to +8 (Reclaimed Woodland Estates (Crops)); farming level +3 (Mast Pasturing (Adaptive husbandry)); farming level +4 (Forest Gardening (Adaptive husbandry)); farming level +7 (Managed Woodland Pastures (Adaptive husbandry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Jewellery Crafter (Industry)); trade income +3 to +4 (Artisan Jeweller (Industry)); trade income +4 to +5 (Jewellers Quarter (Industry)); trade income +5 to +7 (Jewellery Exports (Industry)); public order (happiness) +4 to +6 (Amphitheatre (Leisure)); public order (happiness) +6 to +9 (Colosseum (Leisure))
  • Numeric effects it withholds — these are granted only where it is absent: farming level +2 (Assart Clearings (Crops)); farming level +3 (Rotational Grove Fields (Crops)); farming level +2 (Mast Pasturing (Adaptive husbandry)); farming level +3 (Forest Gardening (Adaptive husbandry)); farming level +6 (Managed Woodland Pastures (Adaptive husbandry)); trade income +1 (Jewellery Crafter (Industry)); trade income +3 (Artisan Jeweller (Industry)); trade income +4 (Jewellers Quarter (Industry)); trade income +5 (Jewellery Exports (Industry)); public order (happiness) +3 to +5 (Amphitheatre (Leisure)); public order (happiness) +4 to +7 (Colosseum (Leisure))
