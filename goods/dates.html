What it does

Where these answers come from, and what an unestablished effect means

What a good does is read off the clauses the mod conditions on it — a requirement on a building level, on a numeric effect, or on a recruitment line, including the ones reached through the building file's own aliases. Negated clauses count: withholding something is an effect. Where nothing in the mod conditions on a good, the page says no effect established rather than describing what the word suggests.

The mod calls this good dates internally; that is the word the conditions on this page are written against.

  • Lets you build — 3 building levels: Date Palm Plantation (Rural), Palm Wine Maker (Rural), Date & Palm Wine Market (Rural)
  • Numeric effects where it is present — trade income +1 (Farms); trade income +1 (Farms+1); trade income +1 (Farms+2); trade income +1 (Farms+3); trade income +1 (Farms+4); trade income +1 (Irrigation Ditches (Crops)); trade income +1 (Basin Irrigation (Crops)); trade income +1 (Canal Network (Crops)); trade income +1 (Irrigation Aqueducts (Crops)); trade income +2 (Irrigated Great Estates (Crops)); trade income +1 (Qanat Well (Crops)); trade income +1 (Qanat Network (Crops)); trade income +2 (Subterranean Aquaducts (Crops))
