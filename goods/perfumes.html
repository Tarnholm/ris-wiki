What it does

Where these answers come from, and what an unestablished effect means

What a good does is read off the clauses the mod conditions on it — a requirement on a building level, on a numeric effect, or on a recruitment line, including the ones reached through the building file's own aliases. Negated clauses count: withholding something is an effect. Where nothing in the mod conditions on a good, the page says no effect established rather than describing what the word suggests.

The mod calls this good perfumes internally; that is the word the conditions on this page are written against.

  • Lets you build — 3 building levels: Herb gatherer (Urban), Perfume Maker (Urban), Medicine and Perfume Export (Urban)
  • Numeric effects where it is present — trade income +1 (Slash and Burn (Crops)); trade income +1 (Assart Clearings (Crops)); trade income +1 (Rotational Grove Fields (Crops)); trade income +1 (Slope Pasturing (Husbandry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Highland Pasturing (Husbandry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Transhumance (Husbandry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Great Transhumance Roads (Husbandry)); trade income +1 (Reedland Pasturing (Adaptive husbandry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Seasonal Pasture Rotation (Adaptive husbandry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Managed Wetland Pastures (Adaptive husbandry)); population health +3 (Physician's Clinics (Healing)); population health +4 to +5 (Healing Sanctuary (Healing))
  • Numeric effects it withholds — these are granted only where it is absent: trade income +1 (Highland Pasturing (Husbandry)); trade income +1 (Transhumance (Husbandry)); trade income +1 (Great Transhumance Roads (Husbandry)); trade income +1 (Seasonal Pasture Rotation (Adaptive husbandry)); trade income +1 (Managed Wetland Pastures (Adaptive husbandry)); population health +2 (Physician's Clinics (Healing)); population health +3 to +4 (Healing Sanctuary (Healing))
