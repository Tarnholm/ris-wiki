What it does

Where these answers come from, and what an unestablished effect means

What a good does is read off the clauses the mod conditions on it — a requirement on a building level, on a numeric effect, or on a recruitment line, including the ones reached through the building file's own aliases. Negated clauses count: withholding something is an effect. Where nothing in the mod conditions on a good, the page says no effect established rather than describing what the word suggests.

The mod calls this good papyrus internally; that is the word the conditions on this page are written against.

  • Lets you build — 4 building levels: Small Papyrus Plantation (Rural), Papyrus Maker (Rural), Papyrus Press (Rural), Papyrus Supply (Rural)
  • Numeric effects where it is present — trade income +1 (Irrigation Ditches (Crops)); trade income +1 (Basin Irrigation (Crops)); trade income +1 (Canal Network (Crops)); trade income +1 (Irrigation Aqueducts (Crops)); trade income +2 (Irrigated Great Estates (Crops)); trade income +1 (Marsh Drainage Channels (Crops)); trade income +1 (Reclamation Embankments (Crops)); trade income +1 (Drainage Pumps (Crops)); trade income +1 (River Diversion Dyke (Crops)); trade income +2 (Reclaimed land Estates (Crops)); trade income +1 (Reedland Pasturing (Adaptive husbandry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Seasonal Pasture Rotation (Adaptive husbandry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Managed Wetland Pastures (Adaptive husbandry)); public order (law) +1 (Academy (Civic)); public order (law) +2 (Great Academy (Civic)); public order (law) +3 (Library of Pergamum); public order (law) +3 (Plato's Academy); public order (law) +4 to +5 (Court of Law (Civic)); public order (law) +5 to +7 (Magnificent Court of Law (Civic))
  • Numeric effects it withholds — these are granted only where it is absent: trade income +1 (Seasonal Pasture Rotation (Adaptive husbandry)); trade income +1 (Managed Wetland Pastures (Adaptive husbandry)); public order (law) +1 (Library of Pergamum); public order (law) +1 (Plato's Academy); public order (law) +3 to +4 (Court of Law (Civic)); public order (law) +3 to +5 (Magnificent Court of Law (Civic))
