In game: Allows the trading of fish

What it is

SubtypeOrdinary trade good — no special engine handling; it works through trade and through what the mod conditions on it.Tier1Trade value1Groupsnone declared

What it does

Where these answers come from, and what an unestablished effect means

What a good does is read off the clauses the mod conditions on it — a requirement on a building level, on a numeric effect, or on a recruitment line, including the ones reached through the building file's own aliases. Negated clauses count: withholding something is an effect. Where nothing in the mod conditions on a good, the page says no effect established rather than describing what the word suggests.

The mod calls this good fish internally; that is the word the conditions on this page are written against.

  • Lets you build — 3 building levels: Fish Salter (Urban), Salted Fish Market (Urban), Garum Factory (Urban)
  • Numeric effects where it is present — farming level +2 (Reedland Pasturing (Adaptive husbandry)); farming level +4 (Seasonal Pasture Rotation (Adaptive husbandry)); farming level +5 (Managed Wetland Pastures (Adaptive husbandry)); population growth +1 (Trade Port); population growth +1 (Shipwright); population growth +1 (Dockyard)
  • Numeric effects it withholds — these are granted only where it is absent: farming level +1 (Reedland Pasturing (Adaptive husbandry)); farming level +3 (Seasonal Pasture Rotation (Adaptive husbandry)); farming level +4 (Managed Wetland Pastures (Adaptive husbandry))
