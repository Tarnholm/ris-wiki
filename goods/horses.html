What it does

Where these answers come from, and what an unestablished effect means

What a good does is read off the clauses the mod conditions on it — a requirement on a building level, on a numeric effect, or on a recruitment line, including the ones reached through the building file's own aliases. Negated clauses count: withholding something is an effect. Where nothing in the mod conditions on a good, the page says no effect established rather than describing what the word suggests.

The mod calls this good horses internally; that is the word the conditions on this page are written against.

  • Lets you build — 4 building levels: Horse Trainer (Rural), Horse Breeder (Rural), Fine Horses Exports (Rural), Hippodrome (Leisure)
  • Numeric effects where it is present — trade income +1 (Open Field Pasturing (Husbandry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Enclosures (Husbandry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Breeding Farms (Husbandry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Great Droveways (Husbandry)); trade income +2 to +3 (Livestock Estates (Husbandry)); farming level +4 (Nomad Communal Herding (Husbandry)); trade income +1 (Nomad Communal Herding (Husbandry)); farming level +6 (Water Covenants (Husbandry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Water Covenants (Husbandry)); farming level +9 (Breeding Stock Exchanges (Husbandry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Breeding Stock Exchanges (Husbandry)); farming level +11 (Warlord's Reserve Herd (Husbandry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Warlord's Reserve Herd (Husbandry)); may stage races (+1) (Racing Course (Leisure)); may stage races (+1) (Stadion (Leisure)); may stage races (+1) (Hippodrome (Leisure)); may stage races (+1) (Huge Hippodrome (Leisure))
  • Numeric effects it withholds — these are granted only where it is absent: trade income +1 (Enclosures (Husbandry)); trade income +1 (Breeding Farms (Husbandry)); trade income +1 (Great Droveways (Husbandry)); trade income +1 (Livestock Estates (Husbandry)); farming level +3 (Nomad Communal Herding (Husbandry)); farming level +5 (Water Covenants (Husbandry)); trade income +1 (Water Covenants (Husbandry)); farming level +8 (Breeding Stock Exchanges (Husbandry)); trade income +1 (Breeding Stock Exchanges (Husbandry)); farming level +10 (Warlord's Reserve Herd (Husbandry)); trade income +1 (Warlord's Reserve Herd (Husbandry)); may stage races (+1) (Racing Course (Leisure)); may stage races (+1) (Stadion (Leisure))
