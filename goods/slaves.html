What it is

SubtypeSlaves — the engine's own slave resource, created when a settlement is enslaved.Tiernot declared — the mod comments its tier line outTrade value1Groupslive goods, slaves
Depletableyes, exhausted after 10 turns, 1,000 population per unit

What it does

Where these answers come from, and what an unestablished effect means

What a good does is read off the clauses the mod conditions on it — a requirement on a building level, on a numeric effect, or on a recruitment line, including the ones reached through the building file's own aliases. Negated clauses count: withholding something is an effect. Where nothing in the mod conditions on a good, the page says no effect established rather than describing what the word suggests.

The mod calls this good slaves internally; that is the word the conditions on this page are written against.

No effect established in the mod files. Nothing in the mod requires it, excludes it, or keys a number off it. The name is mentioned in slaves.txt, but never as a requirement. Its effect is the engine's, not the mod's: the game creates this resource when a settlement is enslaved, and the depletable, base-turns and population fields declared above are what govern it.

Where it is found

1,313 markers on the map, 1,313 in total quantity, across 1,312 regions.

It is on every region on the map, so its presence says nothing about a region — only a surplus does. 1 region has more than one, and a region page lists it only where there is a surplus.
