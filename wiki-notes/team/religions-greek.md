## Where it is on the map

_No region on the map carries a `rel_greek_…` tag._

## The people it belongs to

_No region's ancestry field names `greek` as a people._

## Who follows it

_No faction states this as its default religion._

_There is no `faction_religion_greek` list, so nothing a settlement builds can test for a faction being Greek. 42 such lists exist for the 53 beliefs._

## What builds it

_No building level scales anything on this belief's strength tier._

### Spread by conversion — none

_Nothing outside the tier table grants this belief._

## What it gates

_**Nothing.** No building level and no recruitment line in the mod is conditioned on a belief. A
belief is a number a settlement carries, not a key that opens anything._

## Unrest and identity

| | |
|---|---|
| Group | hellenic (`hellenic`) — the buildings file tests this group directly, with `faction_religion_group_hellenic` |
| Character trait | `BeingGreek` |
| Unrest against its own group | **0** |
| Unrest against other groups | **0** |
| Hidden at zero presence | yes |
| Unrest message | “Greek culture is causing unrest in this settlement” |

Every one of the 53 beliefs RIS declares sets both multipliers to **0**. No belief in this mod creates unrest against another, whether they share a group or not — the religious tension of the base game is switched off across the board, and what the belief system is used for instead is the numbers above.
